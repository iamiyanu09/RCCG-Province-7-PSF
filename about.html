About Us

Who we are and what we're building together

RCCG Province 7 Pastors' Seed Family (PSF) teens is a community of young people growing together in faith, friendship and purpose.

We gather to worship, learn God's word, support one another and have real fun doing it. Every teen is welcome, whether you're new to church or you've been here your whole life.
